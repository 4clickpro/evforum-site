EV insurance — is it really more expensive, and what are you paying?

EV Forum Team · Started November 25, 2026 · Opening post — no replies yet

Ask a room full of EV drivers what they pay for insurance and you will get a room full of different answers. Some say the quote for their electric car came in noticeably higher than the petrol car it replaced. Others say it barely moved, or even came down. Both stories are real, and that is exactly why the question keeps coming up: there is no single answer, and the number on your renewal letter depends on a lot more than the badge on the car.

Part of the reason is that insurers are still learning. An electric car can cost more to repair after a bump — not because the motor is fragile, but because parts can be harder to source, fewer garages are set up to work on them, and a damaged battery pack is an expensive thing to assess and replace. An insurer pricing that risk for the first time will often price it cautiously. But that is only one side of it: where you live, where the car is parked overnight, how many miles you drive, and your own driving history usually matter just as much, and sometimes more.

There is also the small print, which is where the real surprises tend to hide. Does the policy cover the battery if it is damaged in an accident, or is that a separate question? What courtesy car do you get while yours is in the workshop — and how long is the wait if parts are on back order? Some drivers have found that changing insurer, adjusting the excess, or simply ringing round instead of auto-renewing made a bigger difference than anything about the car itself. Others have found the opposite: every quote came back in the same ballpark, and the car simply costs what it costs to insure.

So this thread is for the honest numbers — not the headline claims, but what drivers are actually paying and what changed it. If your insurance went up when you switched to electric, what did you do about it? If it went down, what do you think made the difference? And if you are still deciding, what are you being quoted? Tell us what worked, what surprised you, and what you would say to someone opening their own renewal letter right now.

Over to you

  1. Did your insurance go up, down, or stay about the same when you switched to an electric car?
  2. What do you think made the biggest difference to your quote — the car, where you live, your mileage, or something else?
  3. Did you change insurer, adjust your excess, or change your cover to bring the price down — and did it work?
  4. Is there anything in the small print — battery cover, courtesy car, repair network — that caught you out?
  5. What would you tell someone opening their first EV insurance renewal right now?
